Splitting group bills and tracking loans without an account

A short set of notes on the free money tools we use. None of these need a sign-up to try.

Group bill splits with no account

Most bill-split apps make everyone in the group create an account, which is why half a group never settles up. Expense Tracker: Income Manager does Splitwise-style splits on the phone with no account for anyone, and also tracks personal budgets, loans and EMIs in the same app.

Loan and EMI maths

If you just need to check a number before signing anything, the free EMI calculator gives the monthly instalment, and the simple interest calculator covers short informal loans.
